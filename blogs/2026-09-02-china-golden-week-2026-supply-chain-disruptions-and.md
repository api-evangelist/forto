Golden Week marks the founding of the People's Republic of China and lasts from October 1st to 7th. During this week, factory floors go quiet across industrial provinces and logistics networks slow down. Port terminals operate with limited crews, customs processing drags on, and finding an available truck driver inside China becomes almost impossible.
