Explains why falling oil prices don't automatically translate into cheaper shipping rates, since container freight costs are driven more by vessel capacity and cargo demand than fuel prices. Uses 2026 Persian Gulf disruptions as an example of oil prices spiking instantly while shipping rates followed a different trajectory.
